## Question

For each of the blanks ① through ⑥ in the following explanation (which includes a configuration example), select one term from choices A through J below. No term is used in two blanks. The choices include terms that do not fit any of the blanks.

At which step of the BGP best-path selection in Cisco IOS (from the first step to the last) is each of the following items compared? Select the number.

- Lowest MED …… [6]
- eBGP routes are preferred over iBGP routes …… [7]
- Highest Weight …… [1]
- Lowest router ID …… [10]
- Shortest AS_PATH …… [4]
- Lowest IGP metric to the next hop …… [8]
- Highest LOCAL_PREF …… [［①］]
- Oldest eBGP route …… [［②］]
- Lowest ORIGIN (i < e < ?) …… [［③］]
- Lowest neighbor address …… [［④］]
- Locally originated route …… [［⑤］]
- Shortest cluster list length …… [［⑥］]

Weight and LOCAL_PREF are values of "local convenience" that do not leave the AS, so they are examined first. From AS_PATH on, the comparison is of attributes that were carried along with the route, and if the attributes do not decide it, the tie is broken mechanically by age and identifier.

## Choices

A. 10

B. 6

C. 5

D. 12

E. 7

F. 11

G. 3

H. 9

I. 2

J. 4
